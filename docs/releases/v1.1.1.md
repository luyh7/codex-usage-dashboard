## 功能 · Features

- 新增 GPT-6.1 Terra 的标准、长上下文和 Fast 价格。<br>Add GPT-6.1 Terra standard, long-context, and Fast pricing.
- Windows、Mac 和 Linux 均可添加多个 Codex 日志文件夹。<br>Add multiple Codex log folders on Windows, Mac, and Linux.
- 导入、导出设备快照，汇总多台设备的用量。<br>Import and export device snapshots to combine usage across devices.
- 保存语言、时区和窗口设置。<br>Remember your language, time zone, and window settings.
